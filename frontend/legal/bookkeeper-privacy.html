Privacy policy — Rec IO QuickBooks integration

Effective date: May 4, 2026 · https://rec-io.com/legal/bookkeeper-privacy.html

This policy describes how the optional Rec IO (rec-io.com) integration with Intuit QuickBooks Online handles information in connection with that integration. It supplements (and does not replace) any broader privacy disclosures that apply to Rec IO generally.

1. What the integration does

The integration may read and write accounting-related data in QuickBooks Online that you authorize through Intuit’s OAuth consent flow, and may combine that with operational data from Rec IO (for example trading or cash-movement records) solely to support bookkeeping workflows you configure.

2. Categories of information

  • Credentials and tokens required to call Intuit APIs (for example OAuth refresh tokens) are stored according to your deployment configuration and should be treated as highly sensitive.
  • QuickBooks Online content that you authorize (such as company profile, accounts, transactions, or attachments depending on scopes granted) may be accessed by the integration.
  • Operational metadata from Rec IO (such as identifiers, timestamps, and amounts) may be processed to prepare journal entries or reconciliations.

3. How information is used

Information is used only to operate the integration for your organization: authentication, synchronization, reconciliation, auditing, and support of errors you report.

4. Sharing

The integration communicates with Intuit’s services as required for QuickBooks Online API access. Rec IO does not sell your QuickBooks data. Other subprocessors depend on how your Rec IO environment is hosted (for example hosting provider or database vendor) and should be listed in your general privacy disclosures where applicable.

5. Retention

Retention periods follow your organization’s policies and technical configuration (for example database backups and logs). OAuth tokens should be revoked or rotated when no longer needed.

6. Security

You are responsible for securing production credentials, restricting access to administrative interfaces, and applying updates to your deployment.

7. Your choices

You may disconnect the integration by revoking app access in Intuit’s account settings and removing stored tokens from your environment.

8. Contact

For privacy questions related to this integration, contact the administrator of your rec-io.com organization, or use the contact channel published on rec-io.com.

This document is provided to satisfy developer-platform disclosure requirements. It is not a substitute for advice from qualified counsel for your specific situation.
